# Playtest checklist: first five minutes

Goal: find out why most players leave in the first 3 minutes. Three to five people is enough to see the main problems.

## Who to ask

- 2 people on a phone (one with an older or cheaper phone if you can), 2 on a computer, 1 more of either.
- People who have never seen the game. Friends of friends are better than close friends. Ages 13 to 25 match the CrazyGames audience.

## Before they start

1. Give them the **View Game** link from the CrazyGames developer portal. Do not give them the preview or QA link.
2. If they can, ask them to record their screen (phones have a built-in screen recorder). It is worth more than your notes.
3. Say only this: "Play this game like you would at home. Say out loud what you are thinking. I can't help you."
4. Then stay quiet. Do not explain controls, do not point at things. If they ask a question, write it down instead of answering.

## What to write down (use a stopwatch)

| Time | What happened |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Game opened |
| | First time they moved the character |
| | First hook grabbed (or "never") |
| | First creature picked up |
| | First creature delivered home |
| | First thing they bought |
| | First time they looked confused, and what they were looking at |
| | The moment they stopped or said "OK, I'm done" |

Also note, yes or no:

- Did the sound start by itself?
- Did the controls feel right? (phone: joystick and jump button; computer: mouse look)
- Did the game stutter or lag?
- Did they read the tutorial text, or ignore it?
- Did they understand the wave?
- On a computer: did they figure out how to look around with the mouse?
- On a phone: did a button or panel get cut off or hard to tap?

## Five questions at the end (ask in this order)

1. "What was the game about, in one sentence?"
2. "What would you do next if you kept playing?"
3. "What was the most annoying or confusing moment?"
4. "What was the most fun moment?"
5. "Would you play it again tomorrow? Why or why not?"

## How to read the results

| What you see | What it probably means | Likely fix |
|---|---|---|
| They quit between 2:00 and 4:00, right after the tutorial | No clear next goal after the tutorial | Add a clear next-goal arrow and a bigger reward at minute 3 |
| They never grab the first hook | The swing is not clear enough | Make the first-hook prompt stronger |
| They do not know how to look around | Mouse look is hidden | Make the hint more obvious |
| Phone: stutter or heat | Graphics too heavy | Lower the default quality on phones |
| Phone: buttons hard to tap | Layout problem | Fix the layout |
| They say "I don't get what to do" | Goal not clear | Rewrite the first-minute prompts |
| They stay and ask for more | The core game works | Work on the thumbnail and the first minute only |

## What to send me

Send the notes (photos of paper are fine) and the screen recordings if you have them, along with the device each person used. I will turn them into a short list of fixes.
