## Title

Swing or Splash

## Short description (one line)

Swing across the sea on ropes, grab weird creatures, and get home before the giant wave hits.

## Description

Swing or Splash is a 3D rope-swinging obby with a collecting twist. Run to the edge, jump, grab a hook and swing from island to island over the open sea. Every wave washes strange creatures onto the islands: a Toastopus, a Crab DJ, a Banana Shark and more than 30 others. Grab as many as you can carry and race home before the next wave arrives. If the wave catches you, it takes everything you are carrying.

Creatures you bring home sit on your pedestals and earn cash every second, even while you are away. Spend it on longer ropes, stronger swings, more carry slots and more pedestals. Longer ropes open new zones with rarer creatures, from the calm Shallows to Stormy Seas, Volcano Bay and the Secret Reef.

### Features

- Smooth rope-swinging physics: time your release for a Perfect Release and chain hooks for bonus cash
- 7 zones with their own obstacles: swaying hooks, spring coral, crumbling stones and geysers
- 35 creatures in 7 rarities, plus Gold, Diamond and Rainbow mutations to complete your Index
- A wave every 70 seconds, with Golden Waves and Big Waves that change what washes up
- Hatch eggs at the Hatchery and pick a Buddy pet that follows you and boosts your earnings
- Rebirth to double your income and climb again faster
- Daily rewards, free gifts and a First Voyage quest line
- Plays on desktop and mobile, with progress saved automatically

## How to play / Controls

**Desktop**

- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Space: jump. Hold Space in the air to grab a hook and swing, release to let go
- Mouse: click the game, then move the mouse to look around (Esc frees the cursor). Q / E also turn the camera
- U: Shop, H: Eggs, G: Free rewards, I: Index, L: Locker, P: Settings, M: Mute

**Mobile**

- Left thumb: drag to move
- JUMP button: tap to jump, hold to grab a hook and swing, release to let go
- Drag the right side of the screen to look around
