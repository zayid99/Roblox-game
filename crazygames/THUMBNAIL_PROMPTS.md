# Thumbnail prompts for ChatGPT image generation

## How to use these

1. Open ChatGPT and attach 2 or 3 images from `crazygames/media/reference/` (real in-game shots) plus one of the finished covers. The attachments keep the character, creatures and colours on-model.
2. Paste one prompt below. Each prompt already says which size to make.
3. Ask for 3 or 4 variations and pick the best. Then say what to change ("bigger wave", "character more to the left").
4. Best results for the title: generate the picture **without any text**, then add the game logo yourself in Photoshop, Illustrator or Canva. AI text is often misspelled. The prompts below are written that way. If you want ChatGPT to try the title, add the optional line at the end of each prompt.

## CrazyGames cover rules to respect

- No text except the game title. Never "Play", "New", "Free", "Updated".
- No borders or frames.
- Must look like the real game: same blocky character, same creatures, sea and islands.
- Nothing that looks like Roblox, Minecraft or another game's characters. The character must be original.
- Sizes: landscape 1920x1080, portrait 800x1200, square 800x800. ChatGPT makes 1536x1024, 1024x1536 and 1024x1024, so resize and crop the result to the exact size afterwards.

## Shared style block (already included in each prompt)

Original blocky voxel-style 3D cartoon character with a teal cap, orange t-shirt and dark blue trousers, simple square limbs, friendly face. Toon-shaded with soft dark ink outlines, bright saturated colours, chunky shapes. Bright turquoise sea with white sparkles, blue sky with puffy low-poly clouds, small floating grass islands with palm trees, red-and-white striped lighthouses, golden ring hooks hanging in the air with ropes. Funny cube-shaped sea creatures with big cartoon eyes: a toast-coloured octopus, a red crab wearing headphones, a yellow shark wearing sunglasses, a green pickle blob, a cookie-brown starfish.

## Prompt 1: Landscape cover (16:9), the main thumbnail

```
Create a wide 16:9 game cover illustration (landscape, 1536x1024) for a 3D rope-swinging game, using the attached screenshots as the exact reference for the character, creatures and art style.

Scene: an original blocky voxel-style cartoon character with a teal cap, orange t-shirt and dark blue trousers swings on a thick rope from a golden ring hook, high above a bright turquoise sea. He is mid-swing, body stretched out, both hands gripping the rope above his head, with an excited open-mouth smile. Under one arm he carries a funny cube-shaped toast-coloured octopus with big cartoon eyes. Stacked on his head is a small red crab wearing headphones.

Behind him, a huge curling tsunami wave with a white foamy crest is chasing him, taller than the lighthouses. Small floating grass islands with palm trees and a red-and-white striped lighthouse lead into the distance. On the nearest island a yellow shark wearing sunglasses and a green pickle blob creature look up in surprise.

Style: toon-shaded 3D render look, soft dark ink outlines, bright saturated colours, chunky simple shapes, strong sense of speed with a few white speed lines, sunny blue sky with puffy low-poly clouds. Dynamic diagonal composition, character large in the left-centre third, wave filling the right side. Leave clean sky space in the top-right for a logo to be added later.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no border, no UI elements. Do not use characters from Roblox, Minecraft or any other existing game.
```

Optional title line: `Add the title "SWING OR SPLASH" in the top-right in a chunky white cartoon font with a thick dark navy outline, two lines, spelled exactly like that. No other text.`

## Extra variations to try (swap the Scene paragraph)

Creature pile.** `Scene: the character runs across a small grass island toward the viewer, laughing, carrying a wobbly tall stack of five funny cube-shaped sea creatures in his arms (toast octopus, crab with headphones, yellow shark with sunglasses, green pickle blob, cookie starfish), while a giant curling wave rises behind the island. A golden ring hook with a rope hangs ahead of him.`

Home base.** `Scene: the character stands proudly on a green home island next to rows of glowing teal pedestals, each with a different funny cube-shaped sea creature on it and gold coins popping out above them. A wooden HOME arch, a striped shop stall and a nest of spotted eggs are behind him. Calm turquoise sea and far islands in the background.`

**C. Golden wave.** `Scene: same swing as the main cover, but the giant wave behind him is shimmering gold with sparkles, and the creatures on the islands glow gold, diamond-blue and rainbow.`

**D. Volcano zone.** `Scene: the character swings between dark rock islands with orange glowing cracks and steam geysers, a small volcano smoking in the background, sea still bright turquoise, carrying a red lobster creature with horns.`

## Fix-up phrases that help

- "Keep the character exactly like the attached screenshot: blocky square head, teal cap, orange t-shirt."
- "Make the wave taller and closer, with a thick white foam crest curling over."
- "Simplify the background. Fewer islands, more empty sky."
- "Make the colours brighter and more saturated, like a toy."
- "Remove all text and letters from the image."
