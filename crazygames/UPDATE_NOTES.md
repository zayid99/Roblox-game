# Update notes

## Big Waves Update (15 Nov 2026)

### Short (for the upload form)

Big update! Shorter waits between waves, a Golden Wave at the start of every session, treasure bottles, surprise sea events, daily missions, a 2x cash boost, and a new Deep Voyage quest line. Late zones are much fairer to cross, and the tutorial is smoother on desktop and phone.

### Full

**New**
- Treasure bottles wash up on the islands. Bring them home to open them.
- Sea events from minute 6: Rainbow Rain, Treasure Fleet, Calm Seas and Feeding Frenzy.
- Daily Missions: three new missions every day in the FREE menu.
- 2x Cash Boost: double cash from creatures you bring home.
- Deep Voyage: a new 10-step quest line after the First Voyage.
- The first wave of every session is a Golden Wave.

**Better waves**
- Much less waiting: the wave rises near you and passes faster, so you are back on the course in seconds.
- Longer warnings in the far zones: 8 seconds in Stormy Seas and Kraken Waters, 12 seconds from Volcano Bay on.
- Every island now has a lighthouse you can reach before the wave hits.

**Fairer swinging**
- Pull back in the air to brake, so you no longer fly past the island.
- Grab the edge: after a swing, you climb up if you land just short.
- Geysers and springs no longer throw you into the sea.
- Stormy Seas to Secret Reef islands are bigger, and moving hooks sway less.

**Faster progress**
- Lagoon unlocks much sooner.
- Free gifts come more often, and each one shows its prize.
- New First Voyage quests that lead you to the next zone.

**Smoother start**
- Desktop: you can click buttons while looking around with the mouse.
- Tips appear one at a time instead of all at once.
- Phone: the LET GO button sits next to JUMP and no longer covers your character.
- Unclaimed gifts and new Index entries remind you once.
